My first review in a long, long time. Mostly because of summer, but also because I had to sit with this book for a bit. I loved it, truly. It was what I was waiting for, coming from Stormlight into Mistborn. While a lot of the smaller details are lost with time, the "Sanderlanch" is very, very real with this one. Every character had a satisfying ending, and every plot point I was focused on was wrapped up rather nicely. If anything, the scope of the world seemed to be the biggest thing I wanted more from especially when Roshar was so expansive. Regardless, it was everything I wanted and a little more. I am beyond excited to begin reading the next part of the Mistborn saga, which I'll be doing with my good friends.
