This rocked. Simple opening, but straight to the point. I liked this one a lot more than the first one, and felt like so much improved that it was totally worth the first one taking its time. I read a few reviews that not much happens in this one either; I slightly agree, but so much of what does happen is entertaining enough to excuse it.

We start right where we left off thankfully, with a small introduction to a character we never see again. This happens every now and then throughout the first book as well, but a few of those characters come back in this one and become more relevant (see Elayne and Min). I'm hoping the characters that didn't get as much time in this one see the light of day in the next. 

Rand is still a stubborn mule, Mat takes a sickly back seat, and Perrin starts to slowly accept the reality of being a furry. We get so much more of Loial, which is tight since he is a welcome change of pace to the sword swinging and his relationship with Rand is so endearing it makes you think he met Matt and Perrin a few days before the events of the book started. Everyone's obsession with making a lord out of Rand is hilarious, which I needed after having to deal with his constant inability to accept his destiny. Only at the very end is it hinted that he's began to slowly own up to this reality, but we're left unsure. His hatred of Morraine and the other Aes Sedai still comes off as arrogant, where Nynaeve still has the most to gain from hating and plotting against Morraine. We do get a lot more of her character as well, and thankfully she's not head over heels in love with Rand like every other female character we're introduced too. 

Speaking of, I get this is the 90's, but the whole *literal* fighting over who gets to marry Rand is crazy. This guy has to be the best looking man the land has ever seen, since without so much as a passing glance and a few words heaps of women fall for him. Either he's gorgeous, or men are extraordinarily ugly. This leaves Nynaeve and some of the Aes Sedai as the most interesting female characters. It's a shame since so many of the male characters have great character development and shine as more of their past and motivations reveal themselves (looking at you Ingtar). 

The last 100 pages got me good, crying on the train type of thing. It's a little daunting knowing I have about 12 books left, and the biggest lingering thread left is Rand finding Fain and dealing with him. We know there are lots of Darkfriends looming from the intro chapter, and it seems like we'll slowly be introduced to them throughout the series. Most of the other threads seem like normal passage of time? Egwene and Elayne need to gain Accepted status, Matt needs to recover, Perrin needs to meet back up with his wolf teacher. We also have to now worry about Rand's sword. I did very light research into the next book and saw it's Perrin focused, which to me is bad ass since he probably has the coolest power. 

Giving this one a strong 4 out of 5, closer to a 5 than a 4.
